# D5 quality-aware diversity

Status: **implemented** (2026-11-22). Manifest:
`data/processed/selections/quality_aware_k50_dup0p99_topiq_lam0p1099_seed5942_n15000.jsonl`,
generated by `scripts/calibrate_quality.py`. The full report is in `reports/data/quality-calibration.json`.

## Summary

D5 asks whether adding an image-quality preference to D3's visual-diversity selection helps
retrieval training. It removes images with clear quality defects, then runs D3's facility-location
selection with a bonus for higher-quality images.

The hard part was finding a quality score that identifies real defects without simply removing
certain kinds of content. Six families of measures were tried:
- classical no-reference scores (NIQE, blur, exposure);
- the same scores restricted to object boxes;
- combinations of those scores;
- CLIP zero-shot prompts;
- two learned models trained on human quality ratings (TOPIQ-NR and MUSIQ).

Only the learned models reliably found defects. The final method uses TOPIQ-NR alone, ranked
across the whole training set. It is a hard filter on the worst 0.25% and a weighted preference
during selection. The weight is the largest one that keeps 90% of D3's coverage advantage over
random.

The approved bar was 95% defect precision. TOPIQ's worst 0.25% did not meet it under a strict
reading of "defect". Andrew accepted it on 2026-11-22 with a broader definition of defect (see
[Final method](#final-method)).

## Final method

Approved by Andrew on 2026-11-22.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Quality measure | TOPIQ-NR (CFANet ResNet-50, trained on KonIQ-10k human ratings), native resolution, ranked globally |
| Defect definition | Visibly degraded **or** content-obscuring. This includes deliberate panning blur and heavy filters, which hide the content a caption describes |
| Hard filter | Short side < 192 px (190 images), then the worst 0.25% of the rest by TOPIQ (295 images, TOPIQ < 0.2060) |
| Diversity term | D3 unchanged: sparse facility coverage over frozen OpenCLIP ViT-B-16 (datacomp), k = 50 graph, 0.99 duplicate groups (medoid only), outlier cap 300 |
| Quality term | Raw TOPIQ score, added as a modular bonus. With one measure, the earlier equal-weighting decision no longer applies |
| λ rule | Largest mean TOPIQ that keeps ≥ 90% of D3's exact mean-coverage gain over random. Coarse grid, then 8 steps of geometric bisection |
| Distinctness gates | ≥ 750 images different from random and ≥ 750 different from D3. Mean TOPIQ above D3, with a bootstrap 95% interval (2,000 resamples, seed 5942) excluding zero. Generation fails rather than forcing swaps |

### Objective

```text
F(S) = coverage(S) / N_train  +  λ · Σ_{i∈S} topiq(i) / 15,000
```

The quality term is modular: each image's bonus does not depend on what else is selected. So F
stays monotone submodular. Lazy greedy also stays exact, because every candidate's bonus is
constant and the stale upper bounds remain valid.

In code, `select_facility_location_numpy(..., modular_gains=λ·(N/15,000)·topiq)` adds the bonus to
each candidate's coverage gain. A unit test checks it against brute-force greedy
(`tests/test_facility_location.py`). λ's raw value cannot be read directly, because the two terms
have very different per-image scales. The sweep below calibrates it.

### Reproduce

```bash
export PATH=$PWD/.pixi-home/bin:$PATH
# 1. Learned IQA scores (isolated env, pinned weights, ~81 min on an RTX 2080)
```

The filter on its own (λ = 0) changes only 1,773 images, so the preference term is what makes D5
meaningfully different from D3. Quality gains flatten past λ ≈ 0.5, while coverage falls below
random.

### What D5 gives up

- **Tail coverage.** The coverage loss is concentrated in the rarest visual regions: p99 distance
  rises from 0.350 to 0.387, while p95 barely moves (0.315 → 0.321). If D5 underperforms D3 on
  unusual val2017 images, this is the first explanation to check.
- **Category mix.** Category TVD is 0.028 vs. D3 and 0.093 vs. the train population (D3: 0.078),
  so D5 is slightly less representative than D3.
- **Filtered content.** The 295 TOPIQ-filtered images lean toward indoor casual snapshots (filter
  category TVD 0.163 vs. the population). They overlap 55 of D3's images and 56 of random's. No
  duplicate-group medoid was filtered.

## What was tried

Category bias is the total variation distance (TVD) between a measure's worst-1% category mix and
the train population. A random 1% sample gives 0.052 on average (maximum 0.065 over 20 draws).
Precision is the share of a 24-image sample from the measure's most extreme band that shows a real
defect. "Relative" ranks each image by robust z-score against its 50 nearest frozen-CLIP neighbors.
"Global" ranks it against all of train2017.

| # | Approach | Best category TVD | Best precision (clear defects) | Why rejected |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | NIQE | 0.071 (relative) | ~7–9/24 | Flags clean skies, product shots, fog. Tracks neither blur (ρ −0.02) nor clipping |
| 2 | Laplacian blur, whole image | 0.120 (relative) | ~15/24 | Best classical measure, but flags shallow depth of field and soft-but-usable photos |
| 3 | Exposure (clipped fraction) | 0.197 (global) | ~2–4/24 | Mostly intentional snow, studio, night and silhouette shots. Dropped as allowed |
| 4 | Object-box blur and exposure | 0.140 | ~8–14/24 | Small boxes are noisy, and the largest box is often a deliberately blurred foreground. No better than whole-image measures |
| 5 | Joint blur ∧ NIQE (relative) | 0.118 | ~9/24 | Agreement is better than chance (47 vs. 12 expected at 1%) but still ~40–70% precision. Food categories over-represented |
| 6 | CLIP zero-shot prompts | 0.094–0.302 | ~13/24 (global blur) | Relative ranking cancels the signal. Globally, it flags casual snapshots |
| 7 | CLIP blur ∧ Laplacian blur | 0.162 | ~12–14/24 | Cleanest pre-learned tail (~85% clear or borderline), still short |
| 8 | MUSIQ (KonIQ) | 0.164 (global) | ~16–17/24 | Close to TOPIQ but slightly lower precision. Requiring agreement with TOPIQ adds nothing |
| 9 | **TOPIQ-NR (KonIQ)** | **0.162 (global)** | **~41–42/48; 47/48 clear or borderline** | **Chosen** |

### Lessons

1. **Classical no-reference scores measure content as much as quality.** Ranked globally, NIQE
   and exposure tails were dominated by sky, snow and plain-background photos (category TVD
   0.20–0.30). They would have removed airplane, kite, bird and skiing images.
2. **Neighbor-relative ranking fixes bias only for content-blind measures.** It brought NIQE's
   TVD down from 0.297 to 0.071, close to random. But it hurt every measure built on semantic
   features (CLIP: precision fell from ~13/24 to ~5/24; TOPIQ: TVD rose from 0.162 to 0.287).
   Those embeddings already encode blur and darkness, so an image's neighbors share its defect and
   the z-score cancels it.
3. **Combining weak detectors did not reach the bar.** Joint rules raised precision only modestly
   while shrinking the tail.
4. **Models trained on human ratings were the only ones near 95%.** TOPIQ's worst 0.25% was
   almost all defocus, camera shake, dark noise and degraded scans. Its borderline cases
   (deliberate panning blur, vintage filters) are spread throughout the band, so a stricter
   cutoff does not remove them. They also obscure what a caption describes, which is why the
   approved definition of defect counts them.
5. **Measure at native resolution.** NIQE after P1's 640×640 resize correlates only 0.64 with
   native NIQE (49% overlap of worst-5% sets). After resizing, it mostly ranks images by how much
   P1 distorts them.

## Design history

### Original decisions (approved 2026-11-21) and what became of them

| # | Decision | Approved choice | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hypothesis | Option B: remove clear defects, then add a calibrated quality preference to diversity selection | Kept |
| 2 | Weighting | Equal weights across the measures that survive the audit | Moot: one measure survived |
| 3 | Dependency | `pyiqa==0.1.15.post2` in an isolated `quality` pixi environment; default environment unchanged | Kept. TOPIQ/MUSIQ weights approved 2026-11-22 |
| 4 | Filter combination | Noncompensatory: reject an image that fails any single test | Moot: one measure |
| 5 | Content bias | Rank relative to CLIP neighbors, not globally | **Superseded**: global ranking for TOPIQ (lesson 2) |
| 6 | Filter cutoffs | Strictest cutoff reaching ≥ 95% truly-defective precision | **Relaxed**: ~88% clear and ~98% clear-or-borderline accepted under the broader definition of defect |
| 7 | Diversity space | Frozen OpenCLIP, D3's k = 50 graph and 0.99 duplicate cutoff | Kept |
| 8 | Quality weight | Largest quality gain keeping ≥ 90% of D3's coverage gain over random | Kept (λ = 0.1099) |
| 9 | Minimum difference | ≥ 750 different from random **and** from D3; fail rather than force swaps | Kept |
| 10 | Outliers | Same cap as D3: bottom-1%-density images fill at most 300 slots | Kept |
| 11 | Reporting | Quality distribution, coverage lost, category coverage, content removed | Kept (see [Result](#result)) |

Other decisions:
- **Resolution excluded.** Resolution is not part of the score. 83.4% of images have a 640-px long
  side, and the interquartile range of pixel count is 272,000–307,200, so resolution mostly encodes
  aspect ratio.
- **Small-image floor (2026-11-21).** Images with a short side under 192 px (190 images, 0.16%) are
  excluded. Below 96 px NIQE cannot score at all. Between 96 and 191 px it rests on one or two
  blocks and is unstable. The floor was kept for TOPIQ so the eligible pool stays the same.
- **Sharpness (2026-11-21).** Re-added after the pilot showed NIQE does not track blur. Exposure
  was allowed to be dropped if intentional scenes dominated its tail, which they did.
- **Object-box fallback.** The approved fallback for relative ranking. It ran anyway for comparison
  (`scripts/score_subject_quality.py`).

### Evidence by test

Precision counts are the assistant's visual judgments from contact sheets in
`reports/data/quality-audit/`. They are ranges, not an annotation study.

#### Pilot (2,000 train images, seed 5942)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| NIQE native vs. after P1 640×640 resize | ρ = 0.64; 49% overlap of worst-5% sets |
| NIQE after P1 vs. P1 aspect-ratio stretch | ρ = 0.41 (native: 0.02) |
| NIQE vs. log Laplacian variance / clipped fraction | ρ = −0.02 / −0.05 |

| Worst 20 by sharpness | ~12–15 genuinely blurred, defocused or dim |

#### Joint blur + NIQE (2026-11-22)

The two relative z-scores are nearly independent (ρ = 0.04).

| Each measure's tail | Images in both tails | Expected if independent | Category TVD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5% | 14 | 2.9 | — |
| 1% | 47 | 11.8 | — |
| 2% | 142 | 47.2 | 0.191 |
| 5% | 585 | 295.2 | 0.137 |

The joint ranking scores each image by the smaller of its two z-scores. Worst 0.25% (295 images,
both z ≥ 2.28), 24-image sheet:
- ~9 clear defects: dim or noisy indoor and night shots, faded scans;
- ~8 borderline;
- ~7 sharp or intentional.

The worst-1% TVD is 0.118, with cake ×1.8, carrot ×2.0 and toaster ×2.3.

#### CLIP zero-shot (2026-11-22)

CLIP-IQA-style antonym prompts (for example "a blurry photo." vs. "a sharp photo.") were scored
against the existing frozen OpenCLIP train embeddings (224-px center crop). Score = cosine to the
defect prompt − cosine to the clean prompt. No new packages or weights were needed.

| Measure | TVD, global | TVD, relative | ρ vs. Laplacian | Worst-0.25% precision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| CLIP blur | 0.236 | 0.144 | 0.18 | global ~13/24 clear, ~7 borderline; relative ~5/24 |
| CLIP composite (blur, focus, noise, JPEG, dark) | 0.229 | 0.168 | 0.11 | global ~10/24 clear, ~8 borderline |
| CLIP overall quality | 0.302 | 0.094 | 0.03 | relative ~1/24: flags mundane snapshots |
| CLIP blur global ∧ Laplacian relative | 0.162 | — | — | ~12–14/24 clear, ~6–8 borderline, ~3 sharp |

#### Learned IQA: TOPIQ-NR and MUSIQ (2026-11-22)

Both models were trained on KonIQ-10k human ratings and scored at native resolution (4,865 s on
an RTX 2080, 0 unscored).

| Check | TOPIQ-NR | MUSIQ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Category TVD, worst 1%, global | 0.162 | 0.164 |
| Category TVD, worst 1%, relative | 0.287 | 0.155 |
| Spearman vs. Laplacian blur / NIQE | 0.37 / 0.16 | — |
| Spearman TOPIQ vs. MUSIQ | 0.76 | |

TOPIQ's global worst-1% over-represents indoor casual-snapshot categories (toothbrush ×2.8,
remote ×2.1, cat ×1.8), not the sky and snow scenes that dominated NIQE.

| TOPIQ global band | Images | Clear defect | Borderline | Sharp / intentional |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–0.25%, 300-px sheet | 24 | ~20–22 | ~2–4 | 0 |
| 0–0.25%, 560-px sheet (new sample) | 24 | 20 | 3 | 1 (silhouette) |
| 0.25–0.5% | 24 | ~16 | ~6 | 2 |
| 0.5–1% | 24 | ~10 | ~10 | ~4 (filters) |

Precision falls quickly past 0.25%, which is why the filter stops there and the rest of the
quality effect comes from the soft preference term.
